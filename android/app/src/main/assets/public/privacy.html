Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Tinkle Trouble stores game progress and settings locally on your device. The browser edition does not include advertising, accounts, analytics, or an online leaderboard. Clearing browser data or uninstalling the app may delete your progress.

Android advertising

The Android edition uses Google AdMob for banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and approximate location for advertising, fraud prevention, and measurement, subject to applicable consent choices.

Where required, Google’s consent form appears before ads are requested. Privacy choices can be revisited in Settings. Rewarded ads are optional; coins are granted only after an earned-reward notification.

See Google’s privacy policy and Google advertising information.

Your controls

Use Settings to reset local game progress, change sound or vibration preferences, and revisit available advertising privacy choices. This game does not collect a name, email address, or account profile from players.
